## Transparent dock marks, version1.5

See `dock-brand-sources.json` for exact source URLs and native conversions. Netflix retains the bundled N geometry. YouTube uses the original play-button silhouette with red/white fills. Prime uses original Amazon blue smile/arrow paths, excluding its wordmark. JioHotstar uses unmodified transparent pixels from its official design-team logo at https://www.jiohotstar.design/logo.png with a native alpha-derived clear-space viewport isolating the genuine silver/gold star. Z5 retains the current logo's original shapes, gradient stops and colors from the Wikimedia SVG mirror, corroborated against ZEE's own identity. Reference rainbow rings and a recolored Hotstar star are not presented as official. All Apps is project-native utility geometry. Logos identify installed app destinations; no provider endorsement is claimed.
